Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need previous business experience?

Previous entrepreneurship experience isn't required to learn about the opportunity. Like any business, however, building skills, consistency and knowledge takes time and effort.

What is dropshipping?

Dropshipping is an e-commerce fulfillment model in which a seller can market products without maintaining traditional retail inventory. The exact process and requirements depend on the platform and supplier.

What products are available?

Product availability can change. Watch the presentation for the current information and contact me if you want clarification about the products available through the Velorian opportunity.

How much can I earn?

There are no guaranteed earnings. Results can vary significantly based on factors including effort, skills, sales ability, market conditions, expenses, time invested and other individual circumstances.

Is this a job?

No. This should be evaluated as a business opportunity, not as a guaranteed job or salary.
