Questions

Training FAQ

Do I need previous selling experience?

No. The training is designed around fundamental social media selling concepts and practical activities. You can start with little or no previous experience.

Do I need a large number of followers?

Not necessarily. A large audience is not the only factor that matters. Building meaningful relationships, creating useful content and communicating clearly can be more valuable than simply focusing on follower numbers.

Which social media platform should I use?

You can apply the principles across platforms such as Facebook, Instagram, TikTok or other social networks. Start with the platform where your intended audience is most active and where you can consistently create content.

Will this training guarantee sales?

No. There are no guaranteed sales or income results. Social media performance depends on many factors, including the offer, audience, content, communication, consistency, market conditions and individual effort.

How much time should I spend each day?

Start with a realistic amount of time that you can consistently maintain. The goal is to build a sustainable routine rather than trying to spend many hours online for a short period.

What should I do after completing the seven days?

Continue practicing the skills. Review your content, conversations and activity, identify what you can improve, and build a consistent weekly social media routine.
